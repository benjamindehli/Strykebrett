Strykebrett is a Logan String Melody II sampled note by note, with the machine's character kept intact and a set of extra controls built around it.

Six drawbars set the levels of the bass and treble registers. Four of them run through an ADSR envelope and an LFO, a sine wave in the treble for smooth swells and a sawtooth in the bass for a rhythmic pulse. A highpass filter from 20 Hz to 2 kHz and a lowpass from 200 Hz to 8 kHz each have frequency and resonance.

The ensemble section reproduces the original's modes:

- Organ, a slow stereo chorus.
- O, three stereo choruses at different speeds and depths.
- Solo, a fast stereo vibrato.
- Acc, ensemble off.

A Drift switch gives every sample its own slight, independent pitch instability, with less of it in the bass than in the treble.

After that come a phaser with colour and rate, a tape echo from a Fulltone Tube Tape Echo recorded twice for stereo, and a room reverb from a Chase Bliss Audio & Meris CXM 1978.

121 samples at 44.1 kHz / 24-bit, embedded in the plugin as lossless FLAC.

## Features

- Logan String Melody II sampled across the bass and treble registers.
- Six drawbars, with an ADSR envelope and an LFO on four of them, sine in the treble, sawtooth in the bass.
- Ensemble modes: Organ, O, Solo and off.
- Drift switch giving each sample independent pitch instability.
- Highpass filter (20 Hz to 2 kHz) and lowpass filter (200 Hz to 8 kHz), both with resonance.
- Phaser with colour and rate.
- Tape echo impulse response, short slapback or long with high feedback.
- Room reverb impulse response, small or large.
- Velocity-controlled amplitude switch.
- 121 samples at 44.1 kHz / 24-bit, embedded losslessly (175 MB source library).
